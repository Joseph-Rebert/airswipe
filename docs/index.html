AirSwipe Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

AirSwipe is a Chrome extension that lets you move through YouTube Shorts and TikTok videos by swiping a finger in the air in front of your webcam.

Camera

AirSwipe uses your webcam only while you have switched the camera on from the extension. Each video frame is analysed on your own computer, inside your browser, to find the position of your hand. Video frames and hand positions are never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere. AirSwipe makes no network requests of its own.

Data collected

None. AirSwipe does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information, browsing history, or usage data. It has no accounts, analytics, or advertising.

Access to websites

AirSwipe runs on youtube.com and tiktok.com only, and only to move to the next or previous video when you swipe. It does not read or change anything else on those pages.

Third-party code

Hand tracking uses Google's open-source MediaPipe library and hand model, which are bundled inside the extension and run entirely on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
